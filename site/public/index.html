Before you ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no trial, no ads, and the code is open source. Hosting your own copy runs on Cloudflare's free plan.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. If you host your own, it sits behind your own private login instead.

Where are my workouts kept?

On your phone. Or, if you host your own, on your own Cloudflare account. Nobody else sees them: no tracking, no ads.

Does it work with no signal?

Yes. The whole workout runs on your phone. If you host your own, finished workouts upload once you're back in signal.

Why isn't it in the App Store?

It's a web app, so there's nothing to download. Three taps above and it's on your home screen, opening full screen like any other app.

Can I bring my old training with me?

Paste a plan from your PT or ChatGPT and each day becomes a routine. Runs come in from Strava's download file, and a training spreadsheet can come in too.

What if I lose my phone?

Save a backup now and then (Settings → Backup): one file with everything in it. Keep it in iCloud Drive and open it on the new phone.

Does it work with my Apple Watch?

Rest alerts buzz your watch. There's no watch app or Apple Health link: those need an App Store app.

What it does

Lifting and running, one app.

Built for the gym floor: big buttons, a rest timer that beeps, and nothing to load when the signal drops.

Log as you train

Tick off sets on your phone between lifts. The screen stays on, the rest timer beeps, and last time's weights are right there.

Plans from your PT or ChatGPT

Paste the plan as text. Each day becomes a routine with suggested weights and reps, and it nudges them up as you get stronger.

PBs that count themselves

Personal bests, estimated one-rep max, charts per exercise, strength levels and a year in review. No spreadsheet required.

Runs, next to gym days

Bring in your runs from Strava, or your whole Strava history from its download file, and see the full week in one place.

Your data stays yours

No account, no tracking, no one selling your squat numbers. Everything lives on your phone, or on a server you own.

Back it all up in one file

Workouts, runs, routines and profile in a single file. Keep it in iCloud Drive, or restore it into your own copy later.

What it does

Lifting and running, one app.

Built for the gym floor: big buttons, a rest timer that beeps, and nothing to load when the signal drops.

Log as you train

Tick off sets between lifts. The screen stays on, the rest timer beeps, and last time's weights are right there.

Plans from your PT

Paste a plan from your PT or ChatGPT. Each day becomes a routine with suggested weights that go up as you do.

PBs that count themselves

Personal bests, estimated one-rep max, charts, strength levels and a year in review. No spreadsheet required.

Runs next to gym days

Bring in your Strava history from its download file and see the whole week, lifting and running, in one place.

Your data stays yours

No account, no tracking, no one selling your squat numbers. It lives on your phone, or a server you own.

Back up in one file

Workouts, runs, routines and profile in a single file. Keep it in iCloud Drive, or bring it with you if you host your own.
